# CEO Vision

Write the idea naturally. It may be only a few sentences describing a problem, frustration, target outcome, or product thought.

The studio must translate this into a complete product direction without requiring the CEO to specify features, stack, screens, colors, animations, architecture, APIs, testing, security, or implementation details.

Example:

> I keep forgetting what I finished today and what is still left. I want an app that helps me organize today's work, see what is done, and know what I should do next.

That is enough to begin the studio process.
