# Student & Free Resources

For any external service, tool, API, hosting, database, monitoring, design resource, AI service, or other dependency, check legitimate free and student options before recommending payment.

The AI may investigate student programs, education licenses, free tiers, credits, and open-source alternatives.

If user action is needed, give a compact request:

```text
Need: <exact service/tool>
Why: <what it enables>
Free/student option: <what exists>
Action: <where to claim/install/connect>
Where to place result: <environment variable/config location>
```

Never request or expose secrets in source control.
